## Media

Default television channels are **procedurally generated** in-browser (canvas animations and a shared-renderer product orbit scene). No third-party video files are bundled or streamed by default.

Optional remote video sources can still be wired via `sourceCatalog.ts`; if you add Blender Foundation open movies or other CC media, record them here.

## Historical product

The Nordmende Spectra Color Studio (1973) is a historical consumer electronics design. This project is an interactive educational recreation and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nordmende or its successors.

## Internal components

Exploded-view internals are an **educational reconstruction**. They are not presented as documented factory drawings of the original chassis.
